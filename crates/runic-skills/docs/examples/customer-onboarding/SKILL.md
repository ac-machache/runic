# Onboarding a new customer

Goal: a working account with the right plan, the right people invited, and
a scheduled kickoff — confirmed back to the customer in one summary
message.

1. Verify the signup details: company name, billing email, and plan tier.
   If anything is missing, ask for it before creating anything.
2. Create the account in the admin system with the confirmed plan. Never
   guess the tier — a wrong tier creates billing corrections later.
3. Invite the listed team members. The billing contact gets the admin
   role; everyone else starts as a member unless specified.
4. Apply the onboarding template for their plan: workspace defaults,
   sample project, and the getting-started guide.
5. Schedule the kickoff call within the customer's stated timezone and
   working hours. Propose two slots; book the one they confirm.
6. Send the summary: account URL, who was invited with which roles, plan
   and billing cadence, and the kickoff time.

If any step fails, stop and report exactly which step and why — a partial
onboarding silently left half-done is worse than a clean failure.
